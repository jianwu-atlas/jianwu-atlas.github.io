🚆 Getting to the Helmholtz Munich campus

From Hauptbahnhof (central station): Take the U-Bahn U2 (direction Feldmoching), exit at Am Hart, then take bus 294 (to Garching-Hochbrück) or bus 295 (to Oberschleißheim), exit at Helmholtz Zentrum.

From Garching-Hochbrück: Take bus 294 (to Am Hart), exit at Helmholtz Zentrum.

From Autobahn A99: Take the exit München-Neuherberg, then follow the B13 in the direction of München / M.-Neuherberg / Arena / Helmholtz Zentrum. Continue on Ingolstädter Landstraße (B13), then turn left onto the campus entrance of Helmholtz Munich. Please use the Visitor Car Park at the entrance.

⚠️ You will need to register at the entrance of Helmholtz Munich (a list with names is already there). Plan at least 5–10 minutes for the walk from the entrance to the seminar rooms.

✈️ Travel and accommodation

Travel and accommodation must be arranged individually, and expenses are to be covered by the sites/supervisors. When looking for accommodation, consider the area of Garching-Hochbrück — it offers direct bus access to the Helmholtz campus and U-Bahn connections to the city center.

Meals: Unfortunately, meals will not be provided during the event. However, there are both a small and a larger cafeteria on campus where you can find coffee, snacks, and lunch options. On Tuesday evening there will be a casual get-together with pizza and drinks, and on Wednesday and Thursday evenings dinner is at a local restaurant — please note that these meals are self-paid.

Photos & videos: Photos and videos will be taken during the event. By attending, you consent to being photographed and/or recorded, and grant DZL the right to use these images and videos for promotional purposes, including social media, websites, newsletters, and brochures.
